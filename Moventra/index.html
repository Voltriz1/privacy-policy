Moventra Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Moventra: Workout Planner is an offline-first workout planning application.

Short version: Your workout plans and history are stored locally on your device. Moventra does not require an account and does not send your workout content to our servers. The app includes Google Mobile Ads, which may collect certain device and advertising information as described below.

1. Information stored by Moventra

Moventra stores the following information locally on your device so that the app can provide its features:

  • Workout plans and exercise selections
  • Workout schedules and completed-workout history
  • Favorite exercises and an active workout session
  • Onboarding status and display preferences such as theme selection

This information is stored in the app’s local database and preferences. Moventra does not transmit this workout content to us or operate a user account system.

2. Export, import, and deletion

Moventra lets you export your locally stored workout information as a file or text that you choose where to save or share. If you import data, Moventra processes it on your device.

You can delete the locally stored personal data at any time using Settings → Delete all personal data. You can also delete the app and its local data using your device’s normal app-uninstall controls. Exported copies may remain wherever you chose to save or share them, and are then controlled by that storage or sharing service.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Moventra uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display and measure advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share with Google and its advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention partners:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and ad clicks
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy use
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Advertising ID controls are available in Android settings, and advertising personalization may also be controlled through Google or device-level ad settings.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Moventra can schedule workout reminders locally on your device. Moventra does not send your reminder schedule or notification content to our servers.

5. Information we do not collect

Moventra does not require or collect your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, camera content, payment information, or login credentials. Workout information remains local unless you intentionally export or share it.

6. Data retention and security

Local workout data is retained on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall Moventra. We do not maintain a server-side copy of that data. We use standard platform storage and rely on the security controls provided by your operating system. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Moventra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service used with Moventra, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s data practices or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and publish the revised policy at the same public URL.
