This Privacy Policy explains how RollChoice: Decision Dice handles information when you use the Android application.

  • Effective 21 July 2026
  • Version 1.0
  • Publisher: MindLuso

At a glance

Privacy by design

RollChoice does not require an account. Your custom choices, decision history and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to MindLuso or included in advertising requests.

1. Scope

This policy applies to RollChoice: Decision Dice for Android, package name com.mindluso.the_dice_app, published by MindLuso. It does not govern third-party websites or services linked from this page.

2. Information stored on your device

RollChoice stores the information needed to provide and personalise its features locally on your device. This may include:

Decision content

Custom list names, choice text and your active decision set.

Decision history

Saved results, list names, choice counts and the date and time of a roll, when history is enabled.

App preferences

Language, colour theme, dice style, haptic-feedback and history-saving settings.

Consent preferences

Advertising privacy choices may be stored on the device by Google's User Messaging Platform.

MindLuso does not operate an account system, cloud database, analytics service or custom backend for this version of RollChoice.

3. Advertising and third-party processing

RollChoice uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices where required. Banner ads appear only on the History and Settings screens.

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings and the way an advertisement is served, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and network information;
  • approximate location inferred from network information;
  • app and advertisement interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • consent and privacy signals.

This processing may be used to provide, measure, personalise and secure advertising, where permitted. RollChoice does not add your custom choice text, list names, roll results or decision history to ad requests.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of how it uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your advertising privacy choices

RollChoice requests current consent information when the app starts and displays a privacy message when required. Where Google makes the option available, you can review or change your advertising choices from Settings > Privacy choices in the app.

The app's core decision features remain usable if advertising is unavailable. The type of ads that can be shown may depend on your consent, location and applicable law.

5. Sharing and sale of information

MindLuso does not sell your custom choices or decision history and does not intentionally share that locally stored content with third parties. Information processed through Google Mobile Ads is governed by Google's policies, your consent choices and applicable law.

6. Device access

RollChoice requires internet access for advertising. This version does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, media files or notifications.

7. Retention and deletion

Local decision lists and preferences remain until you change or delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour. Saved decision history remains until you delete an individual entry, clear all history, or remove the app's local data.

You can disable new history entries and clear existing history from within RollChoice. Advertising and consent information processed by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies and your available privacy controls.

8. Children's privacy

RollChoice is not directed to children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in their country. MindLuso does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

9. International processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising-related information in countries other than the one where you live. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards they apply to international data transfers.

10. Security

Local app information is protected by Android's application sandbox and standard on-device storage. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, but RollChoice limits the information it handles and does not maintain a remote database containing your decisions.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RollChoice's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an updated policy is published.
